Entry for the [What the Elevator Would Say](https://make.fun/bounty/u-eager-stork-81-9647-what-the-elevator-would-say-90844958) bounty on make.fun: the funniest one line a talking elevator could say, in its own voice (max 120 characters).

> Floor 13 doesn't exist, but I stop there anyway, just to watch you panic in the mirror.

## How to run

```sh
node main.ts
```

This validates the line against the gate (1–120 characters, single line, not only whitespace) and writes it to `/output/entry.txt`.

## Why this line

The field is crowded with close-door-button jokes and weary complaints about passengers pressing every floor. This one dodges that angle entirely: a mischievous elevator that stops at a floor that does not exist, purely to watch people panic in the mirror. It is elevator-specific — floors and mirrors could come from no other machine — the voice is playful rather than tired, and the twist lands on the last words.
